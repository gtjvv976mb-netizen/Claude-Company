CoinMarketCat is a Chrome extension that runs a launch sniper in your own browser and signs every buy and every sell with your own Phantom, one approval window per trade. It never holds a key. You set the take-profit, the SOL per trade, the daily budget it will not exceed, and the stock-paired tokens to focus on.

What it does

It is the Claude Company launch sniper's lane — HAWK-AI's — with a person's hand on the signature instead of a burner key.

Watch

Every launch, through the same gates

It listens to pump.fun's own program logs and runs each launch through the executor's entry contract: the curve decoded from its own bytes, the ceiling ladder, the mint audit, the socials filter, the fee rail. What it refuses is decided upstream and vendored, never edited here.

Hover

Ten seconds before the strike

A launch that clears opens a would-have position. Only once it is ten seconds old and still marks at or above its own fill does the lane re-read the curve, rebuild the exact instruction it will sign, simulate it, and ask Phantom. A launch nobody followed is never bought.

Leave

In full, on a rule

The take at 1.5×, the stop you chose, the creator's exit, a 90-second stall or a three-minute clock. Each is one more Phantom window; the lane asks again while the rule still says sell.

Limits

Yours, checked before every buy

SOL per trade, a rolling 24-hour budget, the take, the stop, one live position at a time, and which stock-paired tokens to focus on. Every buy is simulated with a spend ceiling and a delivery floor before Phantom is asked.

Book

A shadow book you can grade

Every launch it evaluates becomes a row in the executor's own research schema. Export it and grade the entry rulers with the command the repository ships; the scorecard is live in the popup.

Honesty

The record beside the switch

The arming screen shows the sniper's own live record — a losing one — and refuses to call any of this an edge. The lane starts Off; you choose Observe before you can choose Execute.

How a buy happens

Nothing is signed that was not built, checked and simulated first, and nothing is sent that Phantom did not sign exactly as asked.

Stock-paired tokens

Solana now carries tokenized stocks — xStocks by Backed: TSLAx, AAPLx, SPYx, GLDx and more — and launches quoted in them exist on pump.fun today. CoinMarketCat is being extended to them, on pump.fun curves and on new pools across the ecosystem, with the quote token you pick.

Read this before you pick one. xStocks are Token-2022 mints with issuer controls: a permanent delegate, a pause switch, a live freeze authority and a transfer-hook slot. The issuer can freeze, pause or claw back the quote asset itself, and Backed's terms restrict who may hold them. A position paid in an xStock carries that risk on top of the launch's own, and the arming screen will say so in these words.

Install

It is not in a store. You build it from the repository and load it unpacked — two commands and one click.

  1. Clone and build: 
    git clone https://github.com/gtjvv976mb-netizen/coinmarketcat
cd coinmarketcat && npm ci && npm run build
  2. Open chrome://extensions, turn on Developer mode, press Load unpacked, choose coinmarketcat/dist.
  3. Open the extension's Options and paste an RPC URL — Helius, Triton or QuickNode. The public RPC refuses browsers.
  4. Open the console in a tab, press Connect Phantom, approve the connection. Keep that tab open; the approval windows come from it.
  5. In the popup choose Observe. Watch the shadow book fill. Export it and grade it with node vendor/executor/grade-entry-gates.mjs --file <export>.
  6. Only then: Execute, at the 0.005 SOL canary, with the sentence the lane prints for your wallet typed into the box.

What it refuses, and what you must know

A stop that needs a click is a weaker stop

Phantom does not let an extension sign for you. Every exit is one approval window; the lane asks again while the rule still says sell, but it cannot press Approve. Close the console tab and it cannot ask at all.

One window at a time

One live position by default. A buy window that sits past 25 seconds is abandoned — a launch that old is not the launch that cleared the gates. A declined buy is never re-asked.

Curves only, until told otherwise

A curve that graduates to a pool cannot be sold by this lane; the row says SELL BY HAND. Pool trading is what the stock-paired work adds.

The manifest is the charter

Permissions are storage, alarms and notifications; the content script matches the console pages only; the injected script is the only web-accessible resource. Widening any of it fails the test suite.

What this is. A user-operated tool that runs in your own browser against your own wallet, one approval at a time. No server receives a key, signs, or can reach it. Nothing here is financial advice, nothing here has an edge until you have graded its book over a real sample, and every position it opens can be sold only by a click you make. Tokenized stocks are subject to their issuer's terms and your jurisdiction's rules.
