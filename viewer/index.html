The same company, twice

One architecture, two skylines.

Claude Tower was built for pump.fun: fifty floors, one desk each, the house at the crown. The Robinhood tower is that building with the same bones and different glass — the same sixteen seats, the same blind analysts, the same red team that has refuted every proposal put to it, retargeted at a chain where a block lands every 100 milliseconds and there is no priority fee to bid.

Nothing in either building holds a key. Every ticket leaves unsigned. The only thing that changes when you walk through a door is which chain your wallet is on.
