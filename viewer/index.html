Independence

The analysts don't read each other

Five agents who share notes produce one opinion wearing five hats. These five see the evidence and nothing else, so when they agree it means something.

Adversarial

Someone is paid to hate it

The Red Team isn't asked for balance — it's asked to destroy the idea. The PM has a required field for how the attack was answered, and "refuted" can never become a trade.
