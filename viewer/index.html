01The desk

One trade, fourteen specialists.

The Scout sweeps six Solana launchpads — 14 search angles, ~357 tokens a pass, kill most of what they find with code that costs nothing, and hand the survivors to five analysts who are not allowed to read each other. Lease a floor and yours copies that desk — filtered by your own risk appetite, your categories, your launchpads. You trade SOL from your own wallet; the desk holds no keys and signs nothing.

Every cycle begins wide. The Scout runs fourteen search angles across pump.fun, letsbonk.fun, bags.fm, moonshot, boop and Meteora, and comes back with roughly 357 unique tokens worth a first look.

Then the cheapest seat does most of the killing. The Screener is deterministic code — no model, no mood, no bill — enforcing hard floors: liquidity, age, wash ratio, and a real buy-then-sell probe that proves an exit actually exists. About 79% of candidates die here, at a cost of nothing.

The survivors go to five analysts — forensics, liquidity, flow, narrative, technical — who work blind to one another and are expected to disagree. That is the design: agreement is only worth something when it wasn't arranged. A full workup costs $1.29 to $1.44 in model time; a whole cycle lands around $4.

The suspicion earns its keep. The desk's best catch was a token showing $1.8 million of daily volume that turned out to be 57,479 trades averaging $31.36 against a $64k reserve — turnover wearing depth's coat. No single seat is credited with the catch; the pipeline is the instrument.

The desk also distrusts its own instruments. One pool once quoted RAY at $4,064.74 while the real price was $0.81; every price the desk uses is now a median taken across the deepest pools. When a number is surprising, the ruler is the first suspect.

Whatever remains walks the gauntlet: the Red Team attacks it, the PM may not propose until it states how the Red Team was answered, and Compliance — code, not a model — can veto it before the CEO ever sees it. Only the CEO turns research into a call, and what leaves the building is an unsigned ticket. Always.
