Creator fees

pump.fun pays a coin's creator fee to the wallet that created the coin. This page reads what has accrued and builds the claim — you sign it, in your own wallet. Nothing on this server holds a key, and neither does the bot.

Claim it

Your wallet builds and submits the transaction. The page only supplies the instructions — four of them when both sides hold something: collect from the bonding curve, open a wrapped-SOL account, collect from pump-amm into it, then close it so the lamports arrive spendable.

The claim's account layout is derived, never hardcoded, and is checked against three transactions that landed on mainnet. The two programs spell the same seed differently — creator-vault on the bonding curve, creator_vault on pump-amm — and both derive a perfectly valid address, so the wrong one owns nothing and a claim against it silently takes zero.
