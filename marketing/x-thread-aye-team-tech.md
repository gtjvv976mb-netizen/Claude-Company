1/ THE AYE-TEAM is the house desk on the 50th floor of Claude Tower. Fourteen seats, parody spy names, one question each. Not a committee — an assembly line, and the order of the line IS the technology. 🧵🏭 How a token gets through it, and what the line costs to run:

2/ First seat: the Scout. 🔭 Sweeps 14+ search angles across pump.fun, letsbonk.fun, bags.fm, moonshot, boop and Meteora — ~358 unique tokens a pass. Its job is volume, not judgment. Judgment is expensive on this line. It comes later. ⏳

3/ Second: the Screener. Not a model — pure code. It kills ~79% for free on hard floors: liquidity, age, wash ratio, and a REAL buy-then-sell exit probe at size. An actual quote, not a mid price. 🪓 Models can be argued with. Floors can't. 🧱

4/ Why the floors: markets lie in production. $1.8m of "daily volume" = 57,479 trades averaging $31.36 against a $64k reserve. Turnover, not depth. 🔬 One pool quoted RAY at $4,064.74; real price $0.81. So we price by MEDIAN across the deepest pools and discard the liars. 🎭

5/ Survivors reach five analysts: Forensics, Liquidity, Flow, Technical, Narrative. They run BLIND. 🕶️ Same evidence bundle, zero shared context, no sight of each other's conclusions. They are expected to disagree — the disagreement is data. 🧪

6/ One epistemic rule from the Narrative seat: it never reports "no story exists" — only "could not verify one." 🔍 An absent fetch is not an absent story. The pipeline records a failed lookup as exactly that: a failed lookup. 📭

7/ Then the Red Team. The seat is named AGENT 48, and the joke name is the job description: a contract killer for theses. 🎯 It gets everything and attacks in bad faith — "why does this trade LOSE money?" Record: 9 refuted, 0 survived. The record is AGENT 48's alone. 😈

8/ Risk reads the five AND the Red Team, then sets size and the exact level that proves the thesis broken. That seat is MINI-MEH — the name is literal: it makes your size smaller. ⚖️ JAMES BOUND, the PM, may not propose until it states on the record how AGENT 48 was answered. 📝

9/ Execution writes the ticket: route, slicing, stop, targets. Always UNSIGNED — the desk is read-only by construction: hard RPC allowlist, no keys, no signing, no custody. ✍️ It can only ever hand you paper. You trade SOL from your own wallet. 🔒

10/ Compliance is deterministic code, not a model. A veto, not advice — a vetoed proposal NEVER reaches BIG M, the CEO seat. The refusal outranks the authorisation. 🛑 We wrote a boss the boss can't overrule. It costs nothing to run. Fitting. ⚙️

11/ Last station: the Scribe. It writes the thesis, AGENT 48's verdict, the size, and exactly where the five disagreed — so every call can be graded later against what was known at the time. 📜 The line doesn't end in a trade. It ends in a record. 🗄️
